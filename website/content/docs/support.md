## Quick links

- **Documentation** — you&apos;re here. Start with [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).
- **FAQ** — common buyer and user questions: [/faq](/faq).
- **Email** — `support@quotedeasy.com`. Pro and Starter customers get a
  reply within one business day. Free users get best-effort replies.
- **Bug reports** — [GitHub issues](https://github.com/muahangngayvn/quoted/issues)
  with steps to reproduce, your WordPress + PHP version, and a screenshot.
- **Billing** — manage your subscription, update card, download invoices at
  [Lemon Squeezy customer portal](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders).

## Security reports

Do not file public GitHub issues for security vulnerabilities. Email
`support@quotedeasy.com` directly with subject `[SECURITY]`. We aim to
acknowledge within 24 hours and patch within 7 days for valid reports.
