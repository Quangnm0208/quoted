Quoted is a WordPress plugin that makes your site readable by AI search
engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. This
guide walks you through the first install.

## What you get out of the box

The Free tier ships fully functional with:

- Auto-generated `/llms.txt` (up to 50 posts)
- Markdown endpoint per post: `/wp-json/quoted/v1/llm/{slug}`
- Detection of 14+ AI bots
- Per-bot allowlist with HTTP 403 + robots.txt sync
- Article + FAQPage JSON-LD schema
- Local dashboard with AI Distribution Score and bot activity feed

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.0 or higher
- PHP 7.4 or higher
- Pretty permalinks enabled (Settings → Permalinks, anything other than "Plain")
