General

What Quoted is, who it's for, and what AI visibility actually means.

What is Quoted?
Quoted is a lightweight AI visibility and content-structure layer for WordPress. It helps expose important WordPress content through /llms.txt, clean Markdown endpoints, AI bot tracking, crawler controls, and SEO-plugin compatible schema.
Is Quoted an SEO plugin?
Quoted is not positioned as another traditional SEO plugin. It complements SEO plugins by helping AI assistants and AI-powered search systems read clearer versions of your site content.
What is AI visibility?
AI visibility means making your business, pages, FAQs, documentation, updates, and expertise easier for AI-powered search and assistant tools to understand, reference, and represent accurately.
Does Quoted guarantee ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, or Google AI visibility?
No. No plugin can guarantee AI visibility, citations, rankings, or traffic. Quoted helps prepare and structure your content so AI systems have clearer source material to read.

Compatibility

Where Quoted sits next to your existing WordPress setup.

Does Quoted replace Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, or SEOPress?
No. Keep your existing SEO plugin for titles, meta descriptions, XML sitemaps, on-page SEO guidance, and search workflows. Quoted adds /llms.txt, clean Markdown, AI bot tracking, crawler controls, and schema that is designed to coexist with common SEO plugins.
Does it work with existing WordPress content?
Yes. Quoted works from your existing published posts and pages. You do not need to move content into a separate CMS or rewrite your site.
Does it support documentation and changelog pages?
Quoted can expose published WordPress pages and posts as AI-readable content. If your documentation or changelog lives in WordPress, it can be part of your AI-readable site guide.

Setup & usage

Installing, configuring, and operating Quoted day-to-day.

Will Quoted slow down my website?
Quoted is designed to be lightweight. The Free plan runs locally, /llms.txt output is cached, and bot detection is a simple user-agent check. It does not require an external SaaS call for the core workflow.
Do I need technical skills?
No. Install the plugin, activate it, and review the generated AI-readable output from WordPress. Advanced users can still inspect endpoints, crawler rules, schema behavior, and settings.
What content should I include?
Start with your homepage, service pages, product pages, pricing pages, FAQs, documentation, changelog or update posts, and high-value educational articles.
Can I edit the generated content?
Your WordPress content remains the source of truth. Edit your posts and pages as usual, and Quoted exposes cleaner AI-readable versions from that content.

Pricing & billing

Plans, payment, and what changes when you upgrade.

Is the Free plan enough?
Free is enough to validate the workflow on one site. It includes /llms.txt for up to 50 posts or pages, Markdown endpoints, AI bot tracking, crawler controls, and basic schema. Paid plans add unlimited coverage, longer history, citation checks, multi-site use, and support.
How does billing work?
Billing is handled by Lemon Squeezy. Starter is $19/month or $190/year for one business site. Pro is $29/month or $290/year for small agency and client-site workflows.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. Your paid plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then the plugin reverts to the Free tier.
Can agencies use Quoted for clients?
Yes. The Pro plan is designed for freelancers and small agencies that want a repeatable AI-readiness setup across client WordPress sites.

Privacy & data

What leaves your WordPress site and what doesn't.

Will my data be sent to Quoted servers?
The Free plan operates locally on your WordPress site and does not send telemetry to Quoted. Paid AI checks use your own provider key where applicable.
How are visitor IPs handled in the bot log?
IPs are hashed before being stored in the local wp_quoted_bot_log table. The plugin records user-agent, timestamp, and hashed IP — nothing else.
